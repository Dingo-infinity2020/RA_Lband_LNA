## SMA-hole finding

The V1.0D 3D render showed one open plated hole at the inner boundary of each lower SMA ground solder land. Drill/footprint audit identified them as 0.35 mm GND stitching vias at `(3.700, 7.550) mm` and `(22.700, 7.550) mm`, exactly on the CON-SMA-EDGE-S lower ground-land boundaries.

Electrically these were valid ground vias, but open vias inside/exactly on a wetted connector solder land are unnecessary and can wick solder. V1.0E removes those two drill hits only.
