# V1.0C audit findings and V1.0D corrective action

## What the JLC screenshot revealed
The JLC viewer recognized only solder-mask and drill layers while the copper artwork appeared as `杂层`/miscellaneous layers. This is primarily a **Gerber layer-identification/export problem**: V1.0C used generic `.gbr` names without X2 `TF.FileFunction` metadata. The SMT pads were not intentionally placed on an inner layer; the V1.0C front-copper file contains the SMT pads and RF traces.

## Independent electrical connectivity audit
A source/geometry audit also found two real top-copper opens on `VIN5` in V1.0C:
1. `VIN5` vertical bus stopped at y=14.000 mm while J3 +5 V pad began at y=14.100 mm: 0.100 mm open.
2. C8 VIN neck stopped at y=11.225 mm while C8 pad began at y=11.325 mm: 0.100 mm open.

V1.0D extends both copper shapes to create 0.100 mm overlap.

## SMA ground transfer
The J1 upper top-side SMA ground land had no direct plane-transfer via in its own connected top-copper island. V1.0D adds a tented copper tongue and a 0.35 mm PTH at (4.25, 11.80) mm, with copper annulus on top and bottom, connecting it directly to the solid inner ground planes.

## QPL9547 NC pins
Qorvo specifies pins 3/4/5/8 as internally NC; they may float or be grounded. V1.0D does **not** require grounding pins 5/8. The exposed backside paddle remains the required RF/DC ground and retains the two dedicated via-in-pad holes.

## Layer packaging correction
V1.0D uses JLC-preferred Protel names plus Gerber X2 metadata:
- `.GTL` top copper
- `.G2L`, `.G3L` inner copper
- `.GBL` bottom copper
- `.GTS`, `.GBS` solder mask
- `.GTP` top paste
- `.GTO` top legend
- `.GKO` outline
- `.XLN` plated drill

**Do not order from the V1.0C Gerber ZIP.**
